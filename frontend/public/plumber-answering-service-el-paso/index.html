Questions

Do I need a new number?

No. Forward the calls you don't answer to the line (on most carriers that's *72 and the number). Customers keep calling the number they already have.

What does it say on a burst-pipe call?

It tells the caller to shut off the main valve first — usually near the meter or where the line enters the house — then takes the address and number and alerts you right away.

Can it handle realtors and property managers?

Yes. It takes the property address, the tenant's contact and access details, and books the visit into the windows you open.

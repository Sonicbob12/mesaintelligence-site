Life at our house

Family raised, every day

Our family

Healthy dogs. Stronger futures.

We're a family in Grafton, Ohio, and our Dobermans live in the house with us — not in a kennel. Mom raised this litter in our living room, and the puppies have been handled, played with and loved by all of us since day one.

We want every puppy to land in a home that's ready for a Doberman: plenty of time, exercise and training, and a family that keeps them for life. That's why we ask every buyer to fill out a short application and talk with us first.

Ask us about the parents — we're happy to share their AKC papers and health testing details, and you're welcome to meet mom in person.

Questions

Good to know

How much are the puppies?

Prices are posted with each puppy above as they go up. Until then, call us and we'll tell you where things stand for the color you want.

When can my puppy come home?

The litter was born September 25, 2026. Puppies go home at 8 weeks, starting the week of November 20.

Can I visit before deciding?

Yes — we'd like you to. Call or apply and we'll set up a time to meet the puppies and mom, or a live video call if you're farther away.

Where are you located?

Grafton, Ohio, in Lorain County — southwest of Cleveland. Puppies are picked up at our home; we'll share the address once your visit is set.

Are the puppies AKC registered?

Yes. Both parents are AKC registered purebred Doberman Pinschers, and every puppy is AKC registered. We'll go over the paperwork with you before you take your puppy home.

What colors are available?

This litter has black & rust, red & rust and blue & rust puppies. Check the board above for who's still available.

What about health?

Every puppy has had age-appropriate shots and deworming, is microchipped, and has had its tail docked and dewclaws removed. Ask us about the parents' health testing — we're happy to walk you through it.
